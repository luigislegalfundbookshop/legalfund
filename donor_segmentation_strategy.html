Segment-Specific Outreach Strategy

Major Gift Champions

Direct Outreach: Personal calls, custom proposals, event-based giving opportunities

Timing: 24-48 hours after major legal/media events

Event-Driven Supporters

Digital Campaigns: Rapid response emails, social media amplification, SMS alerts

Timing: Real-time during breaking news events

Quiet Sustainers

Relationship Building: Monthly giving programs, personal updates, impact reports

Timing: Consistent weekly/monthly touchpoints

Strategic Activists

Organizer Engagement: Coalition events, personal milestones, campaign launches

Timing: Coordinated with organizer-driven initiatives
